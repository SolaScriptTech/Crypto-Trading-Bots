# About

Crypto Trading Bots is a research archive documenting the evolution of multiple automated-trading ideas across Kraken, DXtrade, Bybit, and related market-data workflows.

The repository intentionally retains historical versions because they show changes in architecture, signals, execution, and risk management. It is not organized as a single install-and-run product.

Standalone projects are extracted when they have a cohesive identity and dependency set. Scripts that share modules or serve as historical experiments remain here to avoid creating dozens of incomplete repositories.
